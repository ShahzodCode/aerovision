# Aero Vision

A public team website: everyone can join, and anyone can organize an event under the Aero Vision name.

**Live site:** https://shahzodcode.github.io/aerovision/

## What is on the site
- **Main** – introduction, next events, latest blog posts, open surveys
- **Events** – all, current, upcoming and past events, with registration
- **Blog** – news and stories from the team
- **Request** – join the team, organize an event, ask for an opportunity, share an idea
- **About us** – team members and contact details
- **Admin** (for admins only) – manage events, blog posts, surveys, team, requests, accounts and the site text

Built with Vite, TypeScript and Supabase.
